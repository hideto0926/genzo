Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Darkroom Lab (“the App”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what information the App handles and how it is used.

1. Our approach

  • The App has no account sign-up and never asks for personal information such as your name or email address.
  • The App uses no advertising, analytics or tracking tools.
  • Photos you take and prints you make are stored only on your device and are never sent to the developer's servers.

2. Features and information the App uses

FeatureHow it is usedSent off the device
CameraTo take photos and to recognize your hand held over the enlarger (dodging). Hand recognition runs on the device, and the camera feed is not saved.No

3. Information sent to third parties

To get the temperature, the App sends latitude and longitude (rounded to three decimal places) to the weather service Open-Meteo. If you have not allowed location access, the App instead sends the coordinates of a major city matching your device's time zone (such as Tokyo or London), or nothing if there is no matching city. As with any internet request, Open-Meteo receives standard connection information such as your IP address. For how that information is handled, see Open-Meteo's terms and privacy policy.

The App does not store your location, and the developer never receives it.

4. Information stored on your device

Your negatives, prints, print notes and settings are stored inside the App on your device. You can delete them within the App, and deleting the App removes all of them. If they are included in iCloud or other backups, this follows your Apple backup settings.

5. Changing permissions

You can change camera, Photos and location access at any time in the iPhone Settings app → “Darkroom Lab.” The App's main features work without these permissions (for example, with location off you can set the solution temperature manually).

6. Children's privacy

Because the App does not collect personal information, it does not receive personal information from any user, regardless of age.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page along with the “Last updated” date.
